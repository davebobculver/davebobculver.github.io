Undergraduate Research Assistant — PI: Matthias Morzfeld

March 2024 – September 2026

Scripps Institution of Oceanography, University of California, San Diego, CA

Undergraduate Research Assistant — PI: Falk Feddersen

January 2025 – September 2026

Scripps Institution of Oceanography, University of California, San Diego, CA

  • Extended the coordinate-based neural field framework (developed under Dr. Morzfeld) to nearshore breaking waves, fitting LiDAR scans collected from the sea surface in the nearshore region.
  • Modeled up to 60 s of LiDAR data ( $\approx 10^7$ points) to within the sensors' tolerance (6 cm), producing a continuous, differentiable sea surface that can be evaluated at arbitrary space-time coordinates rather than only where sampled
  • Recovered summary wave statistics, including wave speed and wave height, directly from the learned model in agreement with established analytical methods.
  • First-author paper in collaboration with Matthias Morzfeld and Falk Feddersen, and presented the results to an audience of professional oceanographers.
